  Your question (outbox vs. monitoring): Neither. This finding is not an outbox
  problem. Outbox exists to atomically coordinate an external side‑effect
  (connector/messenger/ERP) with a DB commit — but a terminal validation failure
  produces no external effect (the invariant forbids outbox/connector/mutation
  on a denied output). So there's no broker, no outbox. The correct handling is:
